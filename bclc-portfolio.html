My Story

10+ Years Building Workforce Strategy from the Ground Up

I'm a workforce management practitioner with over 10 years of experience — spanning workforce planning strategy, governance, business analysis, executive-facing hiring decisions, WFM systems implementation, and enterprise capacity planning.

I started in 2016 as an RTA at Conduent, building leadership reports and capacity plans for 500+ employees. I was promoted to WFM Lead, overseeing a team of 5 analysts and reporting operations and HR metrics — attrition, turnover, absenteeism — directly informing leadership's hiring decisions with data.

In 2020, I moved to Concentrix as a Workforce Planning Analyst — a deliberate step down in title, because it opened a much broader scope and growth path. That bet paid off: I was promoted to WFM Specialist, where I owned workforce system implementation and enterprise capacity planning across 10,000+ employees and 22+ portfolios in Brazil. I partnered directly with Talent Acquisition — opening requisitions, defining business needs, and executing hiring decisions alongside them, not just handing off a headcount number.

In 2023, I moved to Vancouver to pursue my Master's, graduating in 2024. That same year I joined Coast Capital Savings, responsible for the entire call centre and customer service function across all BC branches. I rebuilt their forecasting methodology from ~60% accuracy to a stable 90% within 90 days. I also led what-if scenario planning during Coast Capital's merger with two other credit unions — modeling resource optimization and reallocation across the combined organization.

In 2025, I joined Hootsuite as Team Lead of Workforce Management, leading a team of 5 analysts globally and using AI to build new strategies and procedures for the team.

Why BCLC

Social Purpose — The Reason I Applied

Purpose-Driven Career Decision

At this point in my career, I'm not just looking for the next role — I'm looking for a mission worth building towards. BCLC's commitment to returning 100% of net income to the province, supporting health care, education, First Nations, and communities across BC, is exactly the kind of purpose that gives workforce planning real weight and meaning.

I've spent over a decade optimizing workforce operations for corporate performance. I want to bring that same precision, strategy, and leadership to an organization where the output directly benefits people and communities across British Columbia. The work I'd do here — building enterprise workforce plans, partnering with leaders, modeling future workforce scenarios — would have a direct line to BCLC's ability to serve the province.

BCLC's social purpose isn't a footnote — it's the reason I applied. I'm ready to invest in something more meaningful, and I believe this is exactly where my experience can make that kind of impact.
